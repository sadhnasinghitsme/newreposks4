Clubs, Events & Sports

Clubs for every interest, celebrations through the year, and a campus built for sport: life beyond the classroom.

Clubs & activities

Something for Every Interest

Music, dance, robotics, arts and more, plus workshops, trips and camps that build skills beyond the syllabus.

Music

A fully equipped music room for western, classical and vocal training.

Dance

Dance classes led by experienced choreographers, with performances at school events.

Robotics

Robotics with LEGO

Hands-on building and coding in the school’s robotic lab, set up in association with LEGO.

Arts

Art & Craft

Drawing, painting and craft work that let children express their ideas.

Workshops & Exhibitions

Expert-led workshops and seminars, and exhibitions where students present their own working models.

Excursions & Camps

Annual educational trips, camping, rope bridges, net climbs and zorb tunnels that build courage and teamwork.

Sports

Sports Facilities

  • Huge indoor swimming pool
  • Playground spanning over 150 metres, with a skating rink
  • Badminton and lawn tennis courts, and cricket nets
  • Indoor sports room for table tennis, chess and carrom
  • Fitness circuit
Teams

Sports Teams & Inter-House Sports

Academics and sports are two sides of the same coin. Inter-house sports competitions held through the year build fitness, team work and sportsmanship, and give every child a chance to compete for their house.

Students who shine in these competitions are picked for the school teams and represent SKS World School in inter-school events.

Give Your Child a Head Start

Admissions open from Pre-Nursery onwards and in Class XI for all streams. Our team will guide you through eligibility, documents, fees and next steps.
