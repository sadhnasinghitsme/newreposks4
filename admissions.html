How to Apply

Admissions are open from Pre-Nursery onwards, and in Class XI for all streams. The school does not accept any donation for admission.

  1. 1

    Application & Registration

    Register online or at the school office within the specified dates. Forms are available on working days at the Administration Office, 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM.

  2. 2

    Documentation

    Birth certificate, medical fitness and blood-group certificate, parents’ address and ID proof, photographs, and a T.C. from the last school (Class II onwards).

  3. 3

    Pre-Admission Test / Interview

    A small written test at pre-primary level; admission is confirmed after an interaction with the Principal.

  4. 4

    Fee Deposit

    Deposit the fee within the stipulated time on confirmation, or the offer of admission stands cancelled.

  5. 5

    Admission Confirmed

    Collect the admission letter, book list and uniform details, and join the SKS family.

Admission FAQs

Is there an entrance exam?

A small written test is taken for the pre-primary level, and admission is confirmed after an interaction with the Principal.

Is the school co-educational?

Yes. Boys and girls are admitted from Pre-Nursery onwards.

What is the teacher–student ratio?

1:20. The school is particular that each student receives individual care and attention.

Are meals provided?

Meals are available at the canteen on a pay-and-buy basis.

What child safety measures are in place?

CCTV, a soft padded play area, a separate bus boarding lane, SMS alerts to parents and an infirmary.

Does the school provide transport?

Yes. All buses are new, with routes customised to reduce travel time, and every bus is mobile-connected and equipped with GPS and CCTV.

Does the school conduct outbound trips?

Yes, outbound trips are organised every year. They are optional, and the expenses are borne by parents.

Can admission be taken in Class XI?

Yes, all streams are available. A written test is conducted, and the desired stream is subject to the Class X CBSE result.
