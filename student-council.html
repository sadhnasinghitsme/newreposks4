House system

Four Houses

The house system drives the school’s cultural and co-curricular activities and builds team spirit and a sense of belonging. Every student from Class I onwards is allotted a house, and inter-house competitions held through the year cultivate a healthy competitive spirit.

Aravali · Satpura · Shivalik · Nilgiri

The house badge is part of the school uniform on all school days.
