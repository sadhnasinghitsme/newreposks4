About SKS World School

An English-medium, co-educational CBSE school under the SKS Educational & Social Trust.

About SKSWS

One of the best schools in Greater Noida West

SKS World School is an English-medium, co-educational institution that runs under the aegis of the SKS Educational & Social Trust, led by its dynamic and visionary Chairman Shri S.K. Sharma. The school provides CBSE curriculum-based education.

Located in the heart of Greater Noida West and enveloped by multi-storied buildings, the school gives every child individual care and attention at a teacher–student ratio of 1:20. Learning happens by doing, supported by smart classrooms, well-equipped labs and a broad co-curricular programme of music, dance, sport and educational visits.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To provide a nurturing and inclusive learning environment that fosters academic excellence, character development and lifelong learning.

Vision: To be a leading institution that inspires innovation, cultivates a passion for learning, and prepares students for success in an ever-evolving world.

Motto

Knowledge · Wisdom · Success

Harmony and Respect: nurturing children to value diversity and build harmonious relationships.

Honesty and Truthfulness: preparing students for life with honesty in their work and conduct.

Thirst for Excellence: developing positive attitudes and the drive to surpass existing standards of achievement.

Affiliation

Affiliated to CBSE, New Delhi. Affiliation No. 2134098. International School Award 2018–21.
