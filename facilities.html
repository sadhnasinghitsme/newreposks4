Home / Facilities

A Campus Built for Learning by Doing

Labs, smart classrooms, a library, an AC auditorium, an indoor pool and a 150 m playground.

Academic

Well-equipped science labs, a mathematics lab with puzzles and games, a computer lab with the latest software, and a state-of-the-art library.

Arts & Culture

A fully equipped music room for western, classical and vocal training, and dance classes led by experienced choreographers.

Sports & Recreation

Indoor swimming pool, indoor sports room (table tennis, chess, carrom), badminton and lawn tennis courts, cricket nets, skating, a 150 m playground and a fitness circuit.

Safety & Security

Trained guards at every entry point, night-vision CCTV across the school, new buses with experienced drivers, a separate boarding lane and SMS updates for parents.

Give Your Child a Head Start

Admissions open from Pre-Nursery onwards and in Class XI for all streams. Our team will guide you through eligibility, documents, fees and next steps.
