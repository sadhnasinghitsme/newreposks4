CBSE Curriculum Structure

Strictly based on the National Curriculum Framework and the National Education Policy, aimed at learning without burden and moving beyond textbooks.

Stages of Schooling

  • Foundational / Preschool: Pre-Nursery to KG
  • Preparatory: Classes I–V
  • Middle: Classes VI–VIII
  • Secondary: Classes IX–X
  • Senior Secondary: Classes XI–XII, all streams

Curriculum & Syllabus

NCERT textbooks as per CBSE guidelines. English medium, with Hindi, Sanskrit and French; Mathematics, EVS / Science, Social Science, computer education from Class III, and the arts.

Assessment

Continuous individual assessment with no formal testing in KG; periodic assessments, projects and practicals from Class I; a two-term structure for Classes VI–X; terminals, revision and pre-board tests for XI–XII.

Pedagogy

A holistic approach that encourages critical thinking and creativity. Students learn by doing, supported by smart classrooms, subject labs, robotics, documentary-based education and annual educational trips.

Wings

Pre-Primary to Senior Secondary

Rules & regulations

School Rules

Every student reads and signs an undertaking to follow the school rules at the start of each session.

Attendance

  • Students are expected to be punctual and attend regularly; absence is granted only for illness or unexpected circumstances.
  • A student with a communicable disease may return only with a doctor’s certificate of fitness.
  • Leave may be allowed by the Principal on a written application from the parent, through the mobile app or in writing.
  • Half-day leave is not permitted for security reasons. In an emergency, parents must take prior permission, collect the student in person and produce a valid document.
  • No re-tests are held for students absent during tests or examinations.
  • 75% attendance is compulsory for Classes IX–XII and 80% for other classes, as per CBSE norms. Students with 100% attendance receive Appreciation Certificates.

Discipline

Discipline is the bridge between goals and accomplishments. The school aims to create an environment in which everyone can live and work together in a supportive way.

  • Violence of any kind, racial comments, stealing and public displays of affection are not encouraged.
  • Breaking school property, trading on campus, possessing forbidden objects, misbehaving with staff or using abusive language leads to disciplinary action.
  • Persistent violations may lead to expulsion from the school.

Promotion & Detention

At the Preparatory Stage (Classes 1–5), year-end subject grades are based on performance in all Periodic Assessments. A student who does not reach the qualifying grade in a subject gets a chance to take an improvement examination. In medical cases certified by a Registered Medical Practitioner, the Examination Committee may promote the child on the cumulative result.

Remedial Classes

When a student is not doing well in a subject, the school takes extra steps: classes that reteach basic concepts, or additional sessions after school hours. Remedial classes focus on the specific subject a student finds difficult and help build better study habits.

Assembly

Assemblies build a positive school ethos of care for the self, care for others and the pursuit of excellence. They are organised around themes, festivals and awareness programmes of global importance.

Uniform

The proper uniform, identity card and house badge are compulsory on all school days. The uniform may be bought from any uniform shop, but its design and colour must match the school’s specification; the school can suggest shops on request.

Give Your Child a Head Start

Admissions open from Pre-Nursery onwards and in Class XI for all streams. Our team will guide you through eligibility, documents, fees and next steps.
